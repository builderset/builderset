_This page describes Builderset 0.5.0._

At the end an Express server stores email templates, articles, forms, card designs
and popups with everything the [data model](/docs/guides/backend/data-model) asks of a
real app: who made, changed and deleted each row and when, a trash to restore from, a
revision on every manual save and publish, and an activity line for everything that
happens. It refuses an unsafe or stale save with the editor's own issue codes, takes
form responses and popup submissions checked by the same `validateAnswers` and
`validateSubmission` the browser ran, and issues cards to members.

There is no ORM: the queries are written against the tables of the
[SQL schema](/docs/guides/backend/sql), in TypeScript, with `pg`. It needs `express`
5, `pg` and the five kits from npm. For the same routes as Next.js route handlers
with an ORM, see [Next.js and Drizzle](/docs/guides/backend/next-drizzle).

```txt title="src/"
db.ts         the pool, transaction(), insert and update helpers
http.ts       HttpError and the one error middleware
auth.ts       who is signed in: a stand-in for yours
checks.ts     the kits' checks, draft or publish
audit.ts      actor columns, activity, revisions, soft delete and restore
storage.ts    uploaded files on disk
models/       one file per group of tables
routes/       documents, history, forms, cards, popups, library, delivery
server.ts     the app
```

### The checks

Every document is checked before it is stored, with the editor's own rules: a draft
may be unfinished but never unsafe, and publishing refuses every error. A refusal
carries the issue codes, so the editor shows each problem on its block. A card's
publish is its approval for print, so it is checked with `isPrintBlocking`. A saved
section is read the way a paste is, against the kit's own elements, and the data a
card is issued with must fill every field the design prints:

### One router for five builders

`documentRouter(model)` is written once and mounted for emails, articles, forms,
cards and popups.
Each handler reads the request and answers; the work is in a function below it. Lists
answer `{ items, total }`, with `?status=`, `?q=`, `?limit=`, `?offset=`, and
`?deleted=true` for the trash.

### Forms and their responses

The public side takes no sign-in. The answers are checked against the **published**
document, never the draft; a filled trap field is answered as a success and stored
nowhere; and only the `values` that `validateAnswers` keeps are stored. The count goes
up in the same statement that checks `maxResponses`, so two visitors cannot both take
the last place. Your team's side lists the responses, triages them with `PATCH` and
deletes them softly; only your retention job deletes them for good.

### Cards

A card is issued from an approved design only — `422` while it is a draft — and a
member holds one card of a design that is not revoked: the partial unique index
`card_issue_one_valid` refuses a second, answered `409`. The number is drawn at
random and never given out twice, not even after a revoke. One card answers with the
design as it was when the card was issued, filled in by `fillCard`, so a reprint
matches the card in the member's hand:

### Popups

Your site asks which popups may show on a page. The status and the dates are one
indexed question; the page's path and language are the kit's `pathMatches` and
`languageMatches`; `shouldShow` decides the rest in the browser — the audience, how
often, the device. `PopupView`'s `onEvent` adds to today's counters, and
`onSubmit` sends what the visitor typed, checked again with `validateSubmission`
against the live popup. Pausing takes a popup off the site without touching its
document:

<include cwd lang="ts" meta='title="src/routes/popups.ts"'>
  ./snippets/backends/express/src/routes/popups.ts
</include>

On the page, `onEvent` posts `{ event }` to `/api/public/popups/<id>/events` and
`onSubmit` posts `{ values, path, locale }` to `…/submissions`; resolve `false`
when the answer is not `201` and the visitor stays on the popup.

</Step>
