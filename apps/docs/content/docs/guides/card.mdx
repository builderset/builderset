At the end a designer in your back office lays out a card — front and back, in
millimetres, with bleed and a safe area — your server approves it with the same
checks the editor ran, and every member gets their own card: on their account page,
and on a sheet a print shop can cut.

_This page describes Builderset 0.4.0, where the card kit is new. Every element is in
[Card elements](/docs/reference/card-elements); the document, the checks and the
renderer's options are in [Card](/docs/reference/card)._

## The editor

A client component, loaded only on the page that edits
([Frontend setup](/docs/guides/frontend)). `createRestHost` saves through your routes
([The host adapter](/docs/guides/host)); `styles.css` is the editor's whole
stylesheet, the card's own look included:

<include cwd lang="tsx" meta='title="card-edit.tsx"'>
  ./snippets/guides/card-editor.tsx
</include>

- `record.status: 'published'` means the design is **approved** for print. The
  approve button calls your host's `publish`, and your server checks again.
- `records` are members to design with: the canvas shows their data, and the
  preview switches between them. Without any, three example members.
- `memberCount` names the print run ("Print 248 cards"); `onPrint` is yours to
  run it. Without `onPrint`, "Print-ready sheet" opens a sheet of the example
  members in a window, over the browser's print dialog.
- The designer works on a free canvas: drag, resize, rotate, snap to the safe area,
  align, layers, the arrow keys in half millimetres (5 mm with Shift). A bank card has its chip
  spot and stripe marked where ISO puts them.

## Your members' data

A card is designed once and printed once per member, so its words hold variables —
`{{member.name}}`, `{{member.valid_until}}` — that each card fills from its member's
record. Which fields exist is yours to say. Give the editor and your server the same
list:

<include cwd lang="ts" meta='title="card-fields.ts"'>
  ./snippets/guides/card-fields.ts
</include>

A `text` field may go into any words, a QR code or a barcode; a `photo` field only
into a photo frame, its value the picture's address. A record is a flat map from
path to text: `{ 'member.name': 'Arben Hoxha', 'member.photo': '/members/ah.jpg' }`.

## The card on a page

`CardView` draws one side at any size, filled in for one member. It has no browser
code: render it in a Server Component. `card.css` is plain CSS under `.bsc`, with no
reset — safe beside your site's own:

<include cwd lang="tsx" meta='title="member-card.tsx"'>
  ./snippets/guides/card-view.tsx#member
</include>

Without `scale`, a card is drawn at its actual size on a screen; a number is pixels
per millimetre, and `'1mm'` is real size on paper. `assetUrl` turns a stored media
key — a side's picture, an image, a logo file — into its address.

## Printing

`CardPrintSheet` lays every printed side of every member's card out at real size,
with the bleed and crop marks, one page each. Print the page with margins off, or
save it as a PDF and send it to the print shop:

<include cwd lang="tsx" meta='title="app/cards/[id]/print/page.tsx"'>
  ./snippets/guides/card-view.tsx#sheet
</include>

The design carries how it is printed: bleed and safe margin in millimetres, 300 or
600 dpi, CMYK or RGB, material, finish and quantity — the root's `print`
([Card](/docs/reference/card#the-document)). A `wallet` card is never printed: it
has no bleed, no safe area, and is only shown on a screen.

the designer's comfort, yours are the ones that count. Then issue each member's card

- `checkCard` and `check_card` return the same issues with the same codes
  ([Issues](/docs/reference/issues)). Warnings — words outside the safe area, type
  under 6 pt, a QR code under 15 mm — never block; a draft may still name a field you
  have not added yet; approving refuses every error.
- `fieldsUsed` says which of a member's fields the design needs; `fillCard` writes
  them in. Photos keep their field: the picture is the record's, by address.
- The tables — `card_design`, its revisions, and `card_issue`, one row per card made
  out to a member, with its number and status — and their routes are in
  [Data model and API](/docs/guides/backend/data-model#the-documents).

## Templates

The editor's Templates tab offers thirteen designs in six groups — membership, ID and
staff, bank and gift, business cards, event badges, wallet — each a front and a back.
For a "New card" screen of your own, `CARD_TEMPLATES` lists them and
`buildTemplateCard(registry, template)` builds one as a whole document, both from
`@builderset/card/editor`. Their words are in Albanian, and
the designer rewords them in the editor.

## Languages

The editor speaks English, Albanian and German (`locales={{ sq, de }}`, each its own
entry point, `@builderset/card/locales/sq`). A design can carry its words in several
languages with `contentLocales`; pass the same list to `checkCard(…, { locales })`.
The words a screen reader hears on a rendered card — "Front", "QR code: …" — are
`CardView`'s `words` ([Card](/docs/reference/card#the-renderer)).

## Fonts

A card's words are set in Poppins, Sora, Inter, Geist Mono, Georgia or the system's
own. The editor loads the web fonts from Google Fonts; pass `fontPreviews={false}`
when your page loads them itself or its Content-Security-Policy allows no font host
([Security](/docs/guides/security#content-security-policy)). On your site,
`CARD_FONTS_URL` from `@builderset/card/render` is the stylesheet that loads them for
`CardView`.
