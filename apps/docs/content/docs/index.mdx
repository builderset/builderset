## What a kit gives you

<Cards>
  <Card label="@builderset/email/editor" title="The editor">
    A React component that fits your app: your colours, your language, light or dark. It saves
    through an adapter you give it and owns nothing else.
  </Card>
  <Card label="@builderset/email/render" title="The renderer">
    A document to email HTML and plain text, or an article to a page of your site. It runs on your
    server, with no browser.
  </Card>
  <Card label="checkEmail · check_email" title="The checker">
    The same rules in the editor and on your server, in TypeScript and in Python, with the same
    issue codes.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## What your project supplies

- **Storage.** A document is JSON. You keep it in your own database, behind your own API
  ([Backend setup](/docs/guides/backend)).
- **Uploads.** Pictures go wherever your files already go.
- **Sending.** You render an email on your server and send it with your own provider
  ([Sending email](/docs/guides/email)).
