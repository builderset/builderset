Builderset is a set of visual editors — **kits** — for documents your users design:
an **email** kit and an **article** kit today. Each kit is an editor, a renderer and a
checker for one kind of document, built on one shared **engine**.

## What you get

  <Card icon={<BookOpen />} title="The renderer" href="/docs/quick-start/email#send-it">

## What your project supplies

- **Storage.** A document is JSON. You keep it in your own database, behind your own API.
- **Uploads.** Pictures go wherever your files go.
- **Sending.** You render an email on your server and send it with your own provider.
