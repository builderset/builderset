---
title: 'Quick start: article'
description: An article editor in a Next.js app, and the article shown on your site by a Server Component. About 10 minutes.
---

At the end you have an article editor on a page of your Next.js app, and a page of
your site that shows the article — rendered on the server, with your links and your
images. Every file below is from
[`examples/next-shadcn`](https://github.com/builderset/builderset/tree/development/examples/next-shadcn),
which is built and tested on every change.

_This page describes Builderset 0.5.0._

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install

Until Builderset is public, its packages are on GitHub Packages. Set up the `.npmrc`
from the [email quick start](/docs/quick-start/email#install), then:

<include cwd lang="bash">
  ./snippets/install-article.sh
</include>

</Step>
<Step>

### The editor page

As for an email, the page reads the article on the server and hands it to the editor:

<include cwd lang="tsx" meta='title="app/articles/[id]/edit/page.tsx"'>
  ../../examples/next-shadcn/app/articles/[id]/edit/page.tsx
</include>

This one keeps the article **in memory** with `createMemoryHost`: nothing is saved, so
you can see the editor before you write any route. To save, use `createRestHost` and
the routes from the [email quick start](/docs/quick-start/email#your-routes) — the
article kit speaks the same REST shape.

<include cwd lang="tsx" meta='title="app/articles/[id]/edit/article-edit.tsx"'>
  ../../examples/next-shadcn/app/articles/[id]/edit/article-edit.tsx
</include>

`record` is what the editor shows around the document: its status, its address
(`slug`), its language. `siteUrl` is where your articles are published, for the
editor's search preview.

</Step>
<Step>

### Show it on your site

`ArticleRenderer` renders the article in a Server Component: no editor code reaches
this page. Pass `next/link` and `next/image`, and import `content.css`, the
article's own stylesheet:

<include cwd lang="tsx" meta='title="app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx"'>
  ../../examples/next-shadcn/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx
</include>

`resolveMedia` turns a stored picture's key into its address — your CDN or your
storage's public URL.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Done

Open `/articles/kitchen-sink/edit` for the editor with every element, and
`/blog/kitchen-sink` for the same article as your readers see it.
